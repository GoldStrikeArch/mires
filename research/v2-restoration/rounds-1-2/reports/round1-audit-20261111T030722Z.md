## Safety and interpretation corrections

1. Integration calls cleanup::run at link/lane.rs:1363, before summary_check::enforce_with at approximately 1505. Later demotion cannot restore already deleted exception edges. opt/witness.rs accepts some NoThrow categories without independently reconstructing their semantic justification. The ledger's 3b-gate finding is substantiated by source inspection; this audit did not rebuild its deliberate mutated linkers.
2. Wrong-typed host input is not only a pending 4'a problem. Retained on-BASE traces already show arithmetic skipping a destructor when a declared u64 receives a Number. Explicit checked boundaries or a real unsafe/trusted declaration distinction are needed before extending this policy. Separately, the fresh pure-Rust as_flattened counterexample needs no dishonest host declaration.
3. Pre-existing must name a baseline. A defect predating one pending optimization can still have become newly observable relative to the restored R0. The reference-field regression previously passed in the assembly and now fails on integration. This audit did not bisect its introducing change.
4. Source analysis shows the current string memo and cheap encoders are useful, but they do not constitute full restoration of union-string/literal-pinning behavior. Builder microbenchmark wins have not yet become a demonstrated whole-application selection win.
5. Runtime ratios measure the named applications/contracts, not pure compiler overhead. Search compares different scoring/index algorithms; gzip uses different CRC algorithms. The fflate improvements also include workload host-binding changes. The timing harness documents these facts and A/A limitations.
6. Exact compression scores can legitimately change with runtime text. The important problem is unprotected runtime/allocation tradeoffs and poor attribution, not any text sensitivity by itself.

## Recommended order

Preserve pending worktree diffs and the temporary verification corpus first. Fix the cleanup validation gate and explicit host-boundary policy; verify value/reference/store repairs before widening inlining or pooling. Resolve or explicitly reject the admitted safe-code counterexamples. Then integrate, rebuild all four Rust targets on one candidate, and measure against matched inputs. Preserve parent and correctness-only controls.

Formatter recognition/carrier composition, structural strings, guarded pooling, and bounded output optimizations are the next major recovery targets after the semantic foundation is stable. The public holdout remains absent. The narrower compiler-track decision may defer it, but the original R1 gate should not be marked satisfied without a documented versioned scope change.
